# Data Science Overview

Data science is an interdisciplinary field that uses scientific methods, processes, algorithms and systems to extract knowledge and insights from structured and unstructured data. It combines aspects of computer science, statistics, engineering, and domain-specific knowledge to enable informed decision-making.

## Core Ideas

The core ideas in data science include:

*   **Data**: The raw material that drives the field, which can be structured (e.g., relational databases) or unstructured (e.g., text documents, images).

*   **Data Analysis**: Techniques for extracting insights and knowledge from data.

*   **Machine Learning**: A subset of data analysis that focuses on developing algorithms that enable machines to learn from data.

*   **Data Visualization**: The process of communicating insights and findings through graphical representations.

## Terminology

Common terms used in data science include:

*   **Big Data**: A term describing the large volumes of structured and unstructured data generated by digital devices and systems.

*   **Small Data**: A term describing the smaller, more manageable datasets often encountered in data analysis.

*   **Data Science Pipeline**: The process of collecting, cleaning, processing, modeling, and deploying data insights.

*   **Feature Engineering**: The process of selecting and transforming variables (features) to prepare them for use in machine learning algorithms.

## Relationships

Key relationships between concepts include:

*   Data science is built upon the foundations of statistics, computer science, and domain-specific knowledge.

*   Machine learning algorithms rely on large amounts of data to learn patterns and relationships.

*   Data visualization is used to communicate insights and findings from data analysis.

*   The data science pipeline encompasses multiple stages of data processing, modeling, and deployment.
